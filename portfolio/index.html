About me

who's behind the screen

I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science Engineering, specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, at Chitkara University, Punjab. My interests sit at the intersection of Web Development, AI/ML, and Data Structures & Algorithms — and I chase all three through hands-on projects rather than theory alone.

I build responsive, user-friendly web applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Django, and Flask. Alongside that, I've explored machine learning through projects involving Natural Language Processing, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, Pandas, and NumPy.

I believe in learning by building real things. Two projects anchor that belief: Oaktrek, an e-commerce platform with authentication, an AI chatbot, and payments, and a Resume–Job Matching System that uses TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity to connect resumes with the right roles. Outside of academics, I solve DSA problems regularly to sharpen how I think through a problem before I write a single line of code.
